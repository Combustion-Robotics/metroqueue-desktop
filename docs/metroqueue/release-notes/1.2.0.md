MetroQueue Desktop 1.2.0

Control your phone from the desktop (needs MetroQueue Android 1.4.2+)
- New devices button in the player bar: find MetroQueue phones and TVs on your Wi-Fi, pair with a code and control them from the player bar
- "Play on <device>" moves what you're playing there; "Continue here" takes it back
- When a phone sends playback to itself, the desktop hands over cleanly

Discord voice bot (off by default)
- Bring your own bot: paste its token in Settings → Discord → Voice bot, invite it with one click
- In a server, /join brings the bot into your voice channel and it plays whatever you're listening to; /leave and /np too
- The app must stay open; crossfades and effects aren't mirrored

Requirements
- macOS 15 or later; builds are unsigned
